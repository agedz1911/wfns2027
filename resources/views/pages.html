WFNS 2027 Free Paper Abstract Submission Guidelines

20th World Congress of Neurosurgery | 16–20 November 2027 | Bali Nusa Dua Convention Center, Bali, Indonesia

Overview and key dates

Free paper abstracts for the 20th World Congress of Neurosurgery are submitted online by the deadline, and the presenting author of each accepted abstract must complete paid registration within 10 working days of the acceptance letter. The Congress takes place on 16–20 November 2027 at the Bali Nusa Dua Convention Center (BNDCC), Bali, Indonesia.

All deadlines end at 23:59 Bali time (WITA, UTC+8).

Submission rules

Abstracts are accepted only through the online submission site. Abstracts sent by email, post or any other route will not be considered.

How to submit
  • Create an account on the congress registration and submission site, or log in with an existing account.
  • Complete the submission form for each abstract. A form with missing required fields cannot be submitted.
  • Preview the abstract and proofread it for spelling, grammar and formatting.
  • Submit the abstract and check that it shows as submitted in your account.
  • Keep the confirmation email, which states your abstract number. If it does not arrive within 24 hours, contact freepaper@wfns2027bali.org.

A Word abstract template is available on the congress website to help authors draft their abstract before entering it in the form.

General rules
  • All abstracts must be written and presented in English.
  • There is no limit on the number of abstracts an author may submit.
  • A presenting author may present a maximum of 2 accepted abstracts.
  • Each abstract must name one presenting author.
  • The submitting author must confirm that all co-authors have approved the content.
  • The presentation format selected in the form is a preference only. The Scientific Committee makes the final decision.
  • Abstracts, including co-authors, may be edited and submitted again until 23:59 WITA on 30 June 2027. The Committee will review only the last version submitted before the deadline. No separate confirmation is sent for edits.
  • The last day for any change is the submission deadline itself, 23:59 WITA on 30 June 2027. After that, nothing can be changed: not the text, authors, co-authors or category.
  • Spelling, grammar and formatting will not be corrected by the Committee. Accepted abstracts are published exactly as submitted.

Abstract format

The abstract body has a maximum of 300 words and must use the five headings below. The title, author details and keywords do not count toward this limit.

Item Requirement
TitleConcise and specific, maximum 20 words, in title case. No abbreviations, no ALL CAPITAL LETTERS and no full stop at the end. Example: Outcomes of Early Decompressive Craniectomy in Severe Traumatic Brain Injury
AuthorsFull names, institution and country, entered only in the portal fields
KeywordsMaximum 4
BackgroundWhy the study was conducted
ObjectivesThe question or aim of the study
MethodsStudy design, patients or materials, and analysis
ResultsActual findings, with enough data to support the conclusions
ConclusionsWhat the findings mean for practice or further research

The following are not permitted in the abstract body:

  • Author names, institutions or other details that identify the authors
  • Tables, figures or references
  • Advertising or promotional content. Use generic names; if a trade name is essential, name products from more than one company. The Committee may remove promotional content
  • Unexplained abbreviations; each must be defined at first use, except standard units
  • Statements such as "results will be discussed at the presentation"

All data must be collected and analysed by the submission deadline. Abstracts without completed results will be rejected.

Ethics and disclosure

By submitting an abstract, the authors confirm that the work is their own and meets each requirement below.

  • Originality: The work must not have been published in a peer-reviewed journal or presented at an international meeting before submission. Work submitted to a journal but not yet published is acceptable.
  • Ethics approval: Studies involving patients, human tissue or animals must have the required ethics approval or exemption.
  • Patient privacy: Consent must be obtained where required, and no information may identify a patient.
  • Conflicts of interest: All financial and commercial relationships must be declared in the portal and on the opening slides or ePoster.
  • Artificial intelligence: Any use of AI tools in writing the abstract must be declared, and AI tools cannot be listed as authors.
  • Research integrity: Plagiarism, fabricated data, falsified data or duplicate submission will result in rejection or removal at any stage.

Review and notification

Each abstract undergoes blinded review by neurosurgeons with expertise in its topic category. Reviewers cannot see author names or institutions. The Committee aims for high scientific quality and a wide geographic spread of presenters.

Abstracts are scored on six criteria:

  • Significance for neurosurgery
  • Innovation in ideas, methods or approach
  • Rigour of methods
  • Quality of the findings presented
  • Implications for research, practice or policy
  • Clarity of writing

The acceptance letter is sent by email to the submitting author and states the abstract number and presentation format. It is treated as received on the date it is sent, so authors should check their spam folders. Authors with no decision by 16 August 2027 should contact the Scientific Secretariat immediately.

The Committee's decisions on acceptance, category and format are final and not open to appeal. Acceptance does not cover registration, travel, accommodation or any other cost.

Strict regulations

The presenting author of every accepted abstract must register and pay in full within 10 working days of the acceptance letter, or the abstract is automatically withdrawn. No extension or reinstatement will be granted.

For this rule:

  • Working days are Monday to Friday, excluding Indonesian national public holidays.
  • Counting starts on the first working day after the acceptance letter is sent.
  • Registration is complete only when full payment has been made and confirmed by the Secretariat. A registration form alone does not count.
  • Sponsored authors must have their payment made within the same period.

For example, a letter sent on Monday 16 August 2027 gives a deadline of 23:59 WITA on Tuesday 31 August 2027. This skips the 17 August public holiday and assumes no other holiday falls in that period.

Presentation and publication

Accepted abstracts are presented as oral papers or ePosters, and each presenter receives the session details before the Congress.

Oral presentation: 8 minutes for the talk and 2 minutes for discussion, using 16:9 slides.

ePoster: One landscape digital poster in the template supplied by the Committee.

Content: The presentation must match the accepted abstract and must not contain commercial promotion.

Certificates: Certificates are issued only to authors who present.

By submitting, authors allow the organisers to publish the abstract exactly as submitted, without corrections, in the Congress programme, abstract book, website and app. Authors must have the right to grant this permission.

Withdrawal and contact

Authors may withdraw an abstract by writing to the Scientific Secretariat by 30 September 2027. Registration fees follow the Congress cancellation and refund policy and are not refunded automatically when an abstract is withdrawn or removed.

For abstract questions, contact freepaper@wfns2027bali.org. For registration and payment questions, contact registration@wfns2027bali.org.

Frequently asked questions

Who can submit an abstract?

Any neurosurgeon, resident, medical student or researcher in neurosurgery or a related field may submit. WFNS membership is not required. The presenting author of an accepted abstract must register for the Congress.

Is there a fee to submit an abstract?

No. Submission is free. Paid registration is required only for the presenting author of an accepted abstract.

How do I submit an abstract?

Create an account on the congress submission site, complete the online form for each abstract, preview it and submit. You will receive a confirmation email with your abstract number.

How many abstracts can I submit?

There is no limit on submissions. However, one person may present a maximum of 2 accepted abstracts.

Can I edit my abstract after submitting it?

Yes, until 23:59 WITA on 30 June 2027. This is the last day for any change: after it, nothing can be changed, including the text, authors, co-authors or category. After editing, submit the abstract again. The Committee will review only the last version submitted before the deadline, and no separate confirmation is sent for edits. Errors will not be corrected by the Committee.

Can I add or change co-authors?

Yes, until the submission deadline. A maximum of 15 co-authors may be listed, and no co-authors can be added after 30 June 2027.

Can I submit work that has been presented or published before?

No. Work published in a journal or presented at an international meeting cannot be submitted. Work submitted to a journal but not yet published is acceptable.

Can I include tables, figures, images or references?

No. The abstract must be text only. Figures and tables may be used in the presentation or ePoster if the abstract is accepted.

Can I use trade names or product names?

Use generic names wherever possible. If a trade name is essential, name products from more than one company. Abstracts must not contain advertising or promotional content.

My abstract fits more than one category. Which should I choose?

Choose the category that best matches the main focus of the work. The Scientific Committee may move an abstract to a more suitable category.

Can I choose between oral presentation and ePoster?

You may state a preference in the form. The Scientific Committee decides the final format, and this decision cannot be changed.

How are abstracts reviewed?

Each abstract undergoes blinded review by neurosurgeons with expertise in its topic category. Reviewers score significance, innovation, methods, findings, implications and clarity.

When will I know if my abstract has been accepted?

All submitting authors will be notified by email by 16 August 2027. Decisions on acceptance, category and format are final.

What must I do after my abstract is accepted?

The presenting author must register and pay in full within 10 working days of the acceptance letter. Otherwise, the abstract is withdrawn automatically and no extension will be given.

Can someone else present my abstract?

Yes, but only a co-author listed on the abstract. Request the change in writing to freepaper@wfns2027bali.org by 30 September 2027. The new presenting author must also register.

Can I present online or send a recorded video?

No. All presentations must be given in person at the Congress in Bali.

Will accepted abstracts be published?

Yes. Accepted abstracts will be published in the Congress abstract book, website and app exactly as submitted. Any journal publication will be announced separately.

Will I receive a certificate?

Yes, if you present your abstract at the assigned session. Certificates are not issued to authors who do not present.

Does acceptance include travel, accommodation or registration support?

No. Authors cover their own registration, travel and accommodation.

Who do I contact with questions?

For abstract questions, email freepaper@wfns2027bali.org. For registration and payment, email registration@wfns2027bali.org.
